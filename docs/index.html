Privacy Architecture

Privacy isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

  • ✓
    All AI runs on your device
    LLM inference via llama.rn — no API calls, no cloud model, no OpenAI, nothing.
  • ✓
    OCR stays on-device
    Google ML Kit Text Recognition v2 — on-device only, never Google Cloud Vision.
  • ✓
    AES-256-GCM encrypted storage

  • ✓
    No account required
    No sign-up, no email, no phone number. Just install and use.

    No telemetry or analytics
    We collect zero usage data, crash reports, or behavioral analytics.
  • ✕

    Android cloud backup is disabled. Your case files never sync to Google Drive or iCloud.

Anomaly Detector

Catches what humans miss

A dedicated second AI inference pass scans every line item, clause, and disclosure for six classes of financial and legal violations.

Overcharge
Charge exceeds allowed amount
Insurance-contracted rates, Medicare/Medicaid limits, or price-listed procedures billed above allowed amounts.
Duplicate Charge
Same service billed twice
Identical CPT codes, lab tests, or service descriptions appearing multiple times on the same bill.
Unauthorized Fee
Fee not in your agreement
Administrative fees, facility charges, or surcharges not disclosed in your lease, insurance plan, or service contract.
Illegal Term
Clause violates consumer law
Waiver-of-rights clauses, unlawful eviction terms, FDCPA violations, or clauses prohibited by state tenant law.
Missing Disclosure
Required notice not provided
HIPAA rights notice, No Surprises Act cost estimates, RESPA disclosures, or state-mandated eviction warnings absent from the document.
Unbundling
Services split to inflate billing
Medical procedures billed as multiple separate line items (unbundling) to circumvent global billing rules and increase total charges.
